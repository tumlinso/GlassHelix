# GlassHelix: useful knowledge inside incomplete evidence

## The scientific question

What description of state is sufficient to predict the biological behavior of interest, and what evidence would justify interpreting that description as mechanism? These are related but different questions. Incomplete observations may support predictions or constrain a family of explanations without selecting one unique molecular story.

GlassHelix should retain that partial knowledge rather than conceal it. Whole candidate explanations can share a response, disagree under an intervention, or be indistinguishable over the measured domain. Those relationships are results worth representing.

## Keep the objects distinct

A measurement is an observation of the system, not necessarily its full state. A latent variable can help prediction without being an identified molecule. A supplied model can be observable under its assumptions while multiple models remain observationally equivalent. Population snapshots are not individual cell trajectories. A local equality or derivative comparison does not establish global biochemical equivalence.

These limits constrain interpretation; they do not imply that nothing is learnable. The toolkit should support useful comparisons, sensitivities, response hypotheses and tests of which additional evidence matters.

## Current machinery versus open formulation

The inspected native foundation has typed system/quantity/observation objects, supplied mechanisms, direct-map and selected dynamical execution, finite whole-candidate propagation and observation updates, interrogation/response tools, and replay/binding paths. Its Cellerator dependencies own numerical execution. A finite supplied candidate set is not general mechanism discovery, and a completed synthetic execution trace is not a fitted biological model.

The current learning work should compose scientific hypotheses and evidence with ordinary framework facilities and Cellerator/CelleraTorch operations where structure helps. This does not require GlassHelix to own another tensor engine, compiler, optimizer or storage system. The precise supported learning path is bound to the implementation snapshot rather than declared complete by this design page.

## The intended feedback into execution

Suppose evidence supports a chromatin variable as part of a useful mechanism. Initially its effects may be represented indirectly or repeatedly reconstructed. Giving it an explicit role can let Cellerator represent the variable, its dependencies and a suitable update/measurement interface directly.

The compiler may then avoid rediscovering structure or specialize a justified operation. But the variable's current value may still require inference. Making ontology explicit is not the same as knowing state. New state dimensions can increase cost; savings require actual reusable information or structure.

Promotion should carry evidence, assumptions, model version and scope. Later contradictory evidence must permit revision or a more general fallback. Automatic proposal→qualification→compiler specialization is the long-term loop, not an already-implemented universal learning feature.

## How to read the evidence

Numerical correctness, systems capability, scientific identifiability and biological validity require different evidence. The existing complete-program benchmark measures a bounded supplied-system lifecycle, including response and observation work. Its activity labels do not prove compact active execution. A separate small ambiguity/perturbation demonstration may make the inference question easier to see, but remains a synthetic capability check.
