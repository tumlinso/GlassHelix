## Implemented facilities

    The current CMake graph builds a
    C++20 GlassHelix foundation against
    the published Cellerator 
    native_foundation and 
    indexed_mechanism components; it does
    not compile sibling Cellerator
    sources. Source and native tests
    cover typed scientific contracts,
    assembled mechanisms, dynamics,
    observations, finite whole-candidate
    propagation/updates, interrogation
    and responses. Replay/Python targets
    are conditional on their source
    files. Supplied candidates remain
    joint state–mechanism alternatives
    with provenance and evidence history.

## Experimental or deliberately bounded

    The complete-program CUDA fixture
    exercises bounded supplied-system
    forward/JVP, response, observation
    and inference paths. Its activity
    labels reflect input-map changes, not
    compact active-only execution. The
    measurements are not a comparison
    against an equivalent alternative
    implementation. Earlier GlassEtch,
    incomplete-model, classifier, direct
    sequence and low-level execution
    material remains experimental or
    unclassified; current classification
    is recorded in the historical
    repository map.

## Future intent and outstanding work

    The ML2 integration remains open in
    the project workflow; this snapshot
    does not record a completed general
    learning layer. A composable
    scientific learning path and
    evidence-scoped promotion of
    supported mechanisms into executable
    structure remain goals, not current
    universal functionality.

## Important qualifications

    A finite supplied candidate set is
    not general mechanism discovery, and
    synthetic checks are not biological
    validation. Candidate inference
    currently uses ordinary normalized
    weights; robustness claims must
    respect the learning-review findings
    until qualified repairs land.
    Historical lifecycle measurements are
    bounded, with portable raw evidence
    still awaiting qualification; they do
    not demonstrate general learning
    throughput or compact activity
    execution.
