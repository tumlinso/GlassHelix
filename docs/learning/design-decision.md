# Shared-support learning decision

The first provisional CellTag question is blind population-law learning from time-indexed RNA. Training may use RNA, sample time, replicate and declared forcing labels. Clone assignments and ATAC values are independent validation evidence. Individual histories are not observed by population snapshots. The day-14 perturbation subseries is excluded from the nominal common regime. This choice remains conditional on the real coverage audit; no CellTag observations are currently present locally.

The selected low-cost construction is an effective source-to-target module relation with shared logical support. Source and target axes are explicitly distinct. It is not a claim that latent coordinates name causal genes. For batch b and target j,

    q[b,i] = s[b,i] * x[b,i]
    v[b,j] = sum_{e:dst(e)=j} w[e] * q[b,src(e)]
    y[b,j] = a[b,j] * v[b,j]

E includes distinct logical edges even where endpoints coincide. The global coefficients w are shared learned unknowns; source and target activities s,a may be ordinary Torch functions of permitted context, local unknowns or fixed controls. x is an input/state representation; a measured RNA channel is not automatically a complete dynamical state. A later inference fixture uses shared coefficients and local hidden initial quantities with explicit observation masks.

The activities are effective modulation, without a chromatin interpretation in the RNA-only arm. Scaling can trade between w and activities, source terms can remain unobserved, and different laws may agree on the observation domain. A frozen global law does not turn indistinguishable individual inputs into distinct warranted labels. A nonzero 2x2 relation and a context relation that changes its cross-ratio furnish a counterexample: two-sided diagonal activity cannot express arbitrary per-context edges. Fixed support can omit interactions. A matched unrestricted predictor and snapshot-only information control remain required for any scientific comparison.

The small executable reference compares factorized scatter, per-instance edge materialization and dense sum assembly for the same expression and parameter values. Zero trainable weights/activities remain in gradient support. Cotangents add over repeated model sites and duplicated argument uses; gradients do not perform updates. A masked objective normalizes observed elements once. Double-precision reference derivatives qualify mathematics; FP32 native acceptance is a distinct producer check. No speedup or molecular discovery is asserted.

## Consumer demand and ownership

Representative fixture: B=8 and tail B=3, source width 3, target width 2, E=5 including duplicated endpoints; extend widths only within producer qualification. Native CE owns stable topology, canonical w, forward/transpose and edge contributions. CT exposes registered framework operation, differentiable x and w, lifetime, saved-forward versions and one sanctioned update/publication. Torch supplies activity generators, products, ordinary layers, optimizer arithmetic and autograd composition. GH owns observations, missingness, allowed evidence, inference protocol and scientific identity.

The new BIO relation composition preserves the existing multi-input joint-product TRAIN path. A new kernel is unnecessary for this design. The consumer needs real installed/pinned CT forward/backward and native w training, accumulation before one step, stale-generation rejection, and logical checkpoint restoration after preparation rebuild. Selected-response actions may use an explicitly qualified VJP/analytic chain where general transforms are unsupported. It will not claim unexecuted higher-order, vmap, compile or autocast behavior.

A fixture-tested client with precise missing-input manifest can qualify engineering when biological inputs are absent. Biology remains not_run; public archive descriptions and synthetic fits do not constitute a real reprogramming result. Producer/consumer receipts must pin exact source/build identities and actual tests.
