# History and superseded material

Older plans, design alternatives and experiments remain useful when their date and status are clear. This index distinguishes them from the current [design](../design/overview.md) and [status](../status/current.md).

- [Historical repository map](../repository-map.md): bootstrap-era source classification and reconciliation record; use the current source map for present entry points.
- `src/models/`, `src/incomplete/`, `Classifier/`, and legacy memory/sequence experiments remain present or partly unclassified; review their consumers before any move.
- Native-foundation and ML planning packages may be active or historical according to their own Todo authority; they are not public architecture pages.
- Preserve numerical/performance receipts in place or export portable copies; do not rewrite old observations to match a newer API.

Original measurement receipts and source hashes are not rewritten during document moves. The migration record maps earlier paths to their current explanatory pages. Generated Todo projections remain with their owner; this archive is not another task authority.
